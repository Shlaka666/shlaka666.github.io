Data deletion instructions

Last updated: October 21, 2026

The internal publishing tools of Shlaka Studio are used only by the studio owner, with the owner's own accounts. They store no data about other people.

Remove the tools' access

  • Facebook: Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations: remove "Shlaka Factory". The stored access tokens stop working at once.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions: remove the studio's app.

Ask for deletion

Email adasha97816@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". Any stored token or data linked to your account is deleted within 30 days and you get a confirmation by email.
