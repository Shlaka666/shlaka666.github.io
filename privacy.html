Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the internal publishing tools of Shlaka Studio ("the tools"). The tools are used only by the studio owner, with the owner's own Google accounts.

What the tools access

  • Google Drive: the studio's own video files and work files, to store and move production files.
  • YouTube: the studio's own channels, to upload the studio's videos and read their statistics.

What the tools do not do

  • They do not access anyone else's account or data.
  • They do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties.
  • They do not use Google user data for advertising.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored only on the studio owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google account. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
